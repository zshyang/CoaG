CoaG authoring editor — drag people and their Bézier handles on the ground, pick a camera path, export scene.json, render with editor/render_authored.py

Top view camera at the bottom, forward is up, right is right. Drag a big circle to move that person at the selected keyframe; drag a small circle (selected person) to bend the path. Units = subject heights; one grid cell = one unit.
